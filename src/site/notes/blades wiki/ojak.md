Bones' vice purvery. (They/them). "selects 'prefers not to say' on forms and actually means it." There are rumors that Ojak is more than one person, and they do nothing to quell those rumors. Ojak operates on the roof of a boutique in Nightmarket. They are very secretive, and sit on a shack on the roof. There are ususally some guards hanging out on the roof too to make sure people don't shoplift. He sells clove cigarettes, and other strange little trinkets.
