# overview
- [[blades wiki/demonic ninja turtle\|the last session]] had us secure a return route from the [[blades wiki/deathlands\|deathlands]].
- barely obtained a monkey paw from a house in the [[blades wiki/deathlands\|deathlands]] for [[blades wiki/raffello\|a demon]].

# details
- now that we sort of have a point of entry/exit from the [[blades wiki/deathlands\|deathlands]], we made our way to the [[blades wiki/circle of flame\|circle of flame]] mansion [[blades wiki/raffello\|raffello]] specified.
- appropriately, [[blades wiki/bae\|bae]] decided we should look for a wheelbarrow to prepare for hauling all the loot from the manor.
- unfortunately, three ghosts were hanging out on the balcony above us, and attacked.
- we were all seriously injured, and barely managed to evade them.
- [[blades wiki/pine\|pine]] dodged a ghost attack, barely avoiding shitting himself; [[blades wiki/brax\|brax]] gaslit one to temporarily flee; [[blades wiki/satin\|satin's]] ghost ward was shattered.
- satin frenzied one ghost to fight the one attacking pine.
- pine started mumbling to himself "i don't believe in ghosts! i don't believe in ghosts!" while picking the lock to the rear entrance.
- satin ordered us to do a smash and grab while she fended off two ghosts. one smashed her ghost bottle when she lost concentration.
- we make it into the upstairs, but the floor collapses from under us.
- pine narrowly avoids falling and finds the safe.
- the papers in the safe turned to dust over time and created a dust cloud that nearly prevented pine from finding the monkey paw.
- we make it outside, where satin has taken severe facial damage from a ghost attack, shattering her spirit mask.
- the party, broken and bleeding, barely make it back to [[blades wiki/cobb\|cobb]], who opens the portal home.
- cobb apparently had to pay off the [[blades wiki/deathland scavengers\|deathland scavengers]], who happened upon our exit route.
