# overview
the group decides to shore up our gang defenses due to our war with [[blades wiki/the hive\|the hive]]. to get more money to hire additional protection, we spent the session planning to start a [[blades wiki/deathlands\|deathlands]] smuggling operation in cooperation with the [[blades wiki/deathland scavengers\|deathland scavengers]].
# details
- [[blades wiki/bones\|bones]] orders cigarette and mushroom coffee with cocaine sprinkles
- spent the first part of the session discussing the gang war, and how to proceed.
- one idea was to hire some protection, given that the hive is *for some reason* probably going to try and attack us with hired whispers.
	- note: drowned powder works well against whispers
- to do this we of course need more money
- ideas thrown out include more theft, theft from the hive, gambling (donkeys), and smuggling.
- smuggling was the only idea pursued, because we may potentially have a unique and rapid transit teleport gate to and from the [[blades wiki/deathlands\|deathlands]] in the [[blades wiki/gateway shrine\|gateway shrine]].
- the [[blades wiki/railjacks\|train wankers]] utilize the realm of the guardian of the gate to move people and goods without incident, and we investigated if that was feasible for our group on a smaller scale.
- the answer is "probably" but more research needs to be done on the matter, in particular how the railjerks don't irritate the old god.
- [[blades wiki/bae\|bae]] and [[blades wiki/brax\|brax]] negotiated the start of a mutually beneficial relationship with [[blades wiki/myst\|myst]] of the [[blades wiki/deathland scavengers\|deathland scavengers]]. we have been tasked with moving some goods. we are to meet up with their exporter a few blocks from our deathland shrine. we were also given a password to prove our identity.
	- note: we will need to secure this shrine in the future and definitely hide its use.
- bae met up with an explorer named [[blades wiki/dowler\|dowler]], who was able to provide us with advice on how to travel safely within the [[blades wiki/lost district\|lost district]]. apparently there is some sort of sign network.
- we will be paid 4-6 coin for the test trip (i forget the exact amount)
- the scavengers want us to drop off the goods to their fence in [[blades wiki/crow's foot\|crow's foot]].
	- does the fence pay us?
