# details
- we're at war with the hive because we stole and pawned their stupid [[blades wiki/golden bee\|golden bee]] and crashed their [[blades wiki/stupid golden party\|stupid golden party]]
- they declared war on us and were coming close to finding our secret base, so we decided to throw them off with:
	- multiple, fake bases in [[blades wiki/barrowcleft\|barrowcleft]].
	- a trap base to lure them to violence.
- [[blades wiki/bae\|bae]] went to her architect friend [[blades wiki/august\|august]], got out their thinking cups, and found a suitable region--barrowcleft.
- the [[blades wiki/dire ants\|dire ants]] scouted the area and found a [[blades wiki/skovlander\|skovlander]]-owned [[blades wiki/furniture storage warehouse\|furniture storage warehouse]] overhanging a nearby canal.
- through his contacts, [[blades wiki/brax\|brax]] found out that a local lesbian bar he used to frequent, [[blades wiki/the pearl divers\|the pearl divers]], was disused because the owner, [[blades wiki/marlane\|marlane]] of the [[blades wiki/billhooks\|billhooks]], is currently in prison, so we are using that as an occasional meeting place. i'm sure she won't mind.
- a group of 6 hive assassins were sent after us, and we had the misfortune of meeting up with them in the overhang canal by our trapped, fake base.
- their leader is [[blades wiki/karth\|karth]].
- after narrowly escaping them by boat, karth and crew went to investigate our base, and were extremely busted by a sticky trap set off by [[blades wiki/jinx\|jinx]]'s literal rats and the ghost [[blades wiki/frost\|frost]]. 
- only karth and [[blades wiki/bowie\|bowie]] survived.
- per a prior agreement during the [[blades wiki/pine's hole\|pine's hole]] heist, the crew contacted [[blades wiki/lenny graves\|lenny graves]] to help with the clean up of the bodies of [[blades wiki/the hive\|the hive]], so when the [[blades wiki/spirit wardens\|spirit wardens]] showed up he could capture/kill one of them.
- sadly, our boat, the [[blades wiki/sugar sandwich\|sugar sandwich]], was lost during the canal escape.
